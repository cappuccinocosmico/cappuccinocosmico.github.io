## Ingredients

```yaml
ingredients:
  - qty: 1/4
    unit: cup
    name: lemon juice
  - qty: 1/4
    unit: cup
    name: water
  - qty: 3
    unit: sticks
    name: butter
  - qty: 6
    unit: whole
    name: eggs
    note: "this is not a typo, include the egg whites"
  - name: dijon mustard
    note: "to taste"
  - name: smoked paprika
    note: "to taste"
  - name: cayenne pepper
    note: "to taste"
```

## Instructions

Big hack I found for this was using an immersion blender along with slowly heating it. Best thing I have found for heating is a temperature controlled induction cooktop set to 210 degrees, but a double boiler will totally work and only be a bit more of a pain to use.

Melt the butter and mix in the lemon juice/water before adding the eggs, just because waiting for the butter to melt without scorching the eggs is long and really annoying.
