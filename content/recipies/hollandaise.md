### Recipe

1/4 cup lemon juice
1/4 cup water
3 sticks of butter

6 whole eggs (this is not a typo, include the egg whites)

(to taste)
dijon mustard
smoked paprika
cyyane pepper

### Notes

Big hack I found for this was using an immersion blender along with slowly heating it. Best thing I have found for heating is a temperature controlled induction cooktop set to 210 degrees, but a double boiler will totally work and only be a bit more of a pain to use.

Melt the butter and mix in the lemon juice/water before adding the eggs, just because waiting for the butter to melt without scortching the eggs is long and really annoying.
