---
title: "Chocolate Chip Cookies"
---

## Ingredients

- 220g (2 sticks/1 cup) nice butter
- 300g (1.5 cups) granulated sugar
- 15g (2 teaspoons) molasses
- 2 large eggs + 1 yolk
- 4g (1.25 teaspoons) vanilla extract
- 8oz/200g of 70% cacao nice baking chocolate (bars not chips)
- 330g (2.33 cups) AP flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 7g (1.25 teaspoons) salt
- Flaky salt for topping

## Instructions

1. **Prep**: Chill dough for 30 minutes before baking
2. **Mix wet**: Cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add molasses, eggs, and vanilla
3. **Mix dry**: Combine flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in separate bowl
4. **Combine**: Fold dry ingredients into wet mixture
5. **Add chocolate**: Chop chocolate bars into chunks and fold in
6. **Chill**: Refrigerate dough for 30 minutes (this prevents spreading)
7. **Bake**: 350°F for 12-14 minutes until edges are golden but centers still soft
8. **Finish**: Top with flaky salt immediately after removing from oven

## History

### 2025-12-15 - V3 Refinement

Chilling the dough for 30 minutes fixed the spreading issue from V2. Cookies now have perfect texture - crispy edges with chewy centers. The flaky salt on top adds a nice contrast.

### 2025-12-10 - V2 Modifications

Added extra egg yolk for richness and switched to chopped chocolate bars instead of chips. Used molasses instead of brown sugar for deeper flavor. **Problem**: Cookies spread too much during baking.

### 2025-12-05 - V1 Baseline

Started with basic recipe using 3/4 cup butter, 1 cup brown sugar, 1/2 cup granulated sugar. Used chocolate chips. Result was too cakey and not rich enough.
