---
title: Crisp Powder
---

Mix these spices in these mass ratios. Quantities are mass units.

## Ingredients

```yaml
- qty: 2
  unit: parts
  name: Chili Powder
- qty: 1
  unit: parts
  name: Garlic Powder
- qty: 1
  unit: parts
  name: Onion Powder
- qty: 1
  unit: parts
  name: Spicy Chili Flakes
- qty: 1
  unit: parts
  name: Salt
- qty: 1
  unit: parts
  name: MSG
- qty: 0.5
  unit: parts
  name: Sugar
- name: Oil
  note: enough to suspend all the spice particles
```

## Instructions

1. Add enough oil to suspend all the spice particles.
2. Microwave for around 3 minutes, or until the mixture starts to boil and release gas (should be very obvious).
3. Recipe complete.
